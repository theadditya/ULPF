How ULPF Works (In Plain English)

1

The Heterogeneous Log Problem

Every firewall brand speaks a different language. Cisco says Built, Palo Alto says allow, and Fortinet says action="accept". Comparing them manually is slow and error-prone.

2

Universal Standardization (OCSF v1.2)

ULPF acts like a universal translator. It takes all those conflicting terms and turns them into a single, clean standard: Allowed, Blocked, or Reset.

3

100% Lossless Guarantee

We never throw away your original log. It is stored verbatim alongside a tamper-proof SHA-256 digital fingerprint, so forensic investigators have courtroom-admissible evidence.

4

AI & Machine Learning Ready

Instead of requiring data scientists to clean text files for hours, ULPF extracts 26 mathematical numbers (bytes ratios, port types, risk scores) on every event in 0.12 milliseconds.
